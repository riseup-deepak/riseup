🧱 When a project goes wrong, most people spend the next week explaining why it was not their fault. They are often correct, and it still does not help them.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Write down the part that was yours.**

Even if it was a small part, put it in one sentence.

**2. Say it to your manager before anyone asks you.**

Tell them what you will do differently next time.

**3. Stop making a list of other people's mistakes.**

You will never use that list, and it changes nothing.

**4. Ask what would have let you stop the problem.**

More warning, a different process, an earlier conversation, or more authority.

Being correct about who caused a problem is worth very little at work. The person who says what they will change is the one who gets the next big piece of work.

*What went wrong recently that you have never said was partly yours?*
