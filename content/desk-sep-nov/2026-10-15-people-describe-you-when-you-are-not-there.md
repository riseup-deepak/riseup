🗣 Decisions about your next job are made in meetings you do not attend. What people say about you in those meetings is built from a few things they remember.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Decide the one sentence you want said about you.**

"She fixes the problems nobody else wants."

**2. Check whether your work matches that sentence.**

If it does not, change the work, not the sentence.

**3. Say the same thing in every update you send.**

People repeat what they have heard more than once.

**4. Ask a colleague how they would describe your work.**

The difference between their answer and your sentence is your job for this year.

You cannot control what people say about you. You can make one thing so obvious that it becomes the easiest thing to say.

*What one sentence would you want said about you in a meeting you do not attend?*
