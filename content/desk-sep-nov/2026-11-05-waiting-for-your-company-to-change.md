🚏 The process is broken, the meetings are pointless, and everyone agrees. Nothing changes, and you have been waiting two years for someone above you to fix it.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Pick the one thing that costs you the most time.**

Not the worst thing. The one that takes your hours every week.

**2. Fix your own version of it first.**

Change how your team does it before asking anyone else to change.

**3. Show the result, not the complaint.**

"We cut this from four hours to one. Here is how."

**4. Give yourself a date to decide.**

If nothing has moved in six months, the question is about you leaving, not about them changing.

Some companies will not change while you are there. Knowing which kind you are in is worth more than another year of waiting.

*What have you been complaining about for more than a year?*
