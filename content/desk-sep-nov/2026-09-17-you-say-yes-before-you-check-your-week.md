📆 Someone asks for help and you agree in the moment. Then you look at your week and there is no time left for the work you already promised.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Answer every request with "let me check and come back to you".**

Say it even when you already know you want to help.

**2. Look at what you have promised this week before you reply.**

Count the hours, not the tasks.

**3. Offer a smaller piece instead of refusing.**

"I cannot run it, but I can review your draft on Thursday."

**4. Tell the person the same day.**

Waiting three days to say no leaves them stuck.

Saying yes quickly feels helpful and generous. The person who suffers is usually the one already waiting for work you promised.

*What did you agree to this week without checking your calendar?*
