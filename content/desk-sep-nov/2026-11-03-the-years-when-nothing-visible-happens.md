🌱 In your first years at work, most of what you build cannot be seen. No title change, no announcement, and it is easy to think everyone else is doing better.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Keep a list of problems you have solved.**

One line each, written the week it happened.

**2. Learn the thing your team keeps waiting for.**

Every team has one job that only one person can do.

**3. Ask to be in the meeting where the decision is made.**

Say you want to listen, not speak.

**4. Judge the year by what you can now do alone.**

Not by your title, and not by who was promoted.

Launch Stage, up to about your second year at work, pays almost nothing back at the time. Most of what you learn there is what you are still using in year twelve.

*What can you do this year that you could not do last year?*
