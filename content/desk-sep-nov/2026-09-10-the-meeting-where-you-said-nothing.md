🤐 You had something useful to say in the meeting and you did not say it. It felt like the safe choice at the time.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Decide before the meeting what you will say.**

Write down one point, so you are not deciding while people are talking.

**2. Speak in the first ten minutes.**

The longer you wait, the harder it becomes to start.

**3. Ask a question instead of making a statement.**

"What happens if the vendor is late?" is easier to say than "this plan is risky".

**4. Write down what you did not say.**

After a month, read the list and see how much of it was worth saying.

Saying nothing keeps you comfortable on the day. Over a year, the people who decide your next role only remember the times you spoke.

*What did you not say in your last meeting, and why?*
