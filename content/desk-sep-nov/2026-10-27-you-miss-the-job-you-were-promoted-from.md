🪜 The promotion made sense at the time. A year in, you spend your days in meetings about work you used to do yourself, and you are worse at this job than the last one.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Write down which parts of the new job you avoid.**

Those are the parts you have not learned yet.

**2. Ask for training on one of them.**

Most companies promote people and then teach them nothing.

**3. Keep one piece of the old work every week.**

A few hours, enough to stay good at it.

**4. Say out loud that this is harder than you expected.**

Nothing gets fixed while you are pretending it is fine.

A promotion is a different job, not a bigger version of your old one. Almost nobody is told that before they accept it.

*Which part of your current job have you been avoiding since the day you started it?*
