🎒 Most people put months into getting the offer and nothing into the first ninety days. What you are actually given to do is decided in those ninety days, not in the interview.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Ask your manager what a good first ninety days looks like.**

Ask in week one, while the answer can still change.

**2. Find the person everyone asks for help.**

It is rarely the most senior person, and they will teach you the most.

**3. Finish one small thing in the first month.**

Something visible and complete, not a plan for something large.

**4. Write down what surprised you in week two.**

In six months you will not be able to see it any more.

An offer means somebody decided to take a chance on you. What they decide next is based on your first three months.

*What do you want to be true about you in ninety days?*
