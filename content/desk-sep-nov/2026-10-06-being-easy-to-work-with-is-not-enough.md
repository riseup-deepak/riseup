🤝 Agreeing quickly makes people like working with you. It also means nobody remembers a moment when you changed the outcome.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Disagree once in every long meeting.**

Say what you would do instead, not only what is wrong.

**2. Put your recommendation in writing before the meeting.**

Then there is a record of what you thought before the decision.

**3. Ask for the work you want.**

People give the hard projects to whoever asks first.

**4. Stop apologising when you have done nothing wrong.**

Watch how often you write "sorry" in messages this week.

Being liked and being trusted with a decision are two different things at work. Plenty of people spend ten years with the first one and never get the second.

*When did you last disagree in a meeting and say what you would do instead?*
