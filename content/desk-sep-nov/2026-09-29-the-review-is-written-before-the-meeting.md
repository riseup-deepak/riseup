📄 Most people prepare for their performance review the week before. By then your manager has already written it, using what they remember from the last few months.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Send your manager a short list every month.**

Three lines on what you finished and what it changed.

**2. Use the same words your company uses.**

If the goal is called retention, write retention, not "keeping people happy".

**3. Ask in month two what a strong year looks like.**

Get the answer while there is still time to do it.

**4. Give your manager the examples they will need.**

They have to defend your rating to other managers who have never met you.

A review is a summary of what your manager can remember. Most of that is decided long before anyone books the meeting.

*What has your manager forgotten that you did this year?*
