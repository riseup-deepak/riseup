🧠 You knew within a minute whether you wanted the job, the project or the hire. Everything you did after that was collecting evidence for a decision you had already made.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Write your first answer down before you research.**

Then you can see later how much your view actually changed.

**2. Ask what would have to be true for you to be wrong.**

Write three things down, and go and check each one.

**3. Find one person who disagrees and let them finish.**

Do not answer or explain until they have said everything.

**4. Wait one night on anything that takes a year to undo.**

Job offers, hires, house moves and resignations all qualify.

Fast decisions are usually right about small things. The expensive mistakes are the ones that felt obvious within a minute.

*What did you decide quickly this year and then spend weeks justifying?*
