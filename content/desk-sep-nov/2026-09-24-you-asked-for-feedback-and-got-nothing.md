📝 You ask how you are doing and hear "you are doing well". It is pleasant, it is useless, and you leave knowing exactly as much as before.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Ask about one piece of work, not about yourself.**

"What would have made Tuesday's proposal better?"

**2. Ask what you should do more of and less of.**

Two answers, both usable.

**3. Ask the person who saw the work, not your manager.**

The colleague who sat through that meeting saw more than your manager did.

**4. Say thank you and change one thing.**

People stop giving feedback to those who argue with it.

Most people are willing to help you and unwilling to criticise you. A narrow question about one piece of work makes it safe for them to answer.

*What is the last specific thing anyone told you to do differently?*
