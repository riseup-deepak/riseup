🧭 Many people wait for their manager to notice what is slowing them down. Managers are busy, and they usually notice only when the work is late.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Say what is blocking you in one sentence.**

"I cannot finish this until finance approves the budget."

**2. Bring the answer with the problem.**

Say what you want them to do, so the decision takes one minute.

**3. Tell them how you want to hear that something is wrong.**

In the moment, or in your one to one, but say which.

**4. Ask what they are being measured on this year.**

Then you know which of your work actually helps them.

Most managers are not refusing to help. They are waiting to be told exactly what would make a difference.

*What has been slowing you down that your manager does not know about?*
