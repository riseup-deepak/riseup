🔁 You do not decide how carefully you work. After two years beside people who reply late and leave problems unfixed, you start doing the same.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Name one person at work whose habits you would not want.**

You already know who it is, and you have started working the same way.

**2. Count how much time you spend with that person each week.**

Count the meetings, the messages and the lunches across one ordinary week.

**3. Find one person who prepares better than you do.**

Ask to be put on something small with them, even for two weeks.

**4. Copy one thing they do, starting this month.**

How they prepare for a meeting, or how quickly they answer a request.

Nobody decides to work less carefully. It happens slowly, by sitting next to it every day.

*Whose working habits have you picked up without choosing to?*
