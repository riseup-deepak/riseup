🛠 AI now does the first draft, the summary and the analysis. The work still gets done, and the part of you that used to do it gets slower every month.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Pick one skill you will keep doing by hand.**

The one your job would be hard to explain without.

**2. Do the first version yourself once a week.**

Rough is fine. The point is the practice, not the output.

**3. Check the AI output against something you know is true.**

If you cannot check it, you cannot defend it.

**4. Write down where the AI was wrong.**

After a month you will see the pattern, and the pattern is where you are still needed.

Using AI well and staying good at the work are two separate jobs. Only one of them shows up in this week's output.

*Which part of your work could you no longer do without help?*
