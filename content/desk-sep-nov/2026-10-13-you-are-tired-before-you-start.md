🔋 Burnout does not arrive on one bad day. It arrives after months of weeks that were slightly too full, and by then a weekend of rest no longer fixes it.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Notice the small things you have stopped doing.**

Exercise, cooking, replying to friends. These go first.

**2. Count how many evenings you worked last month.**

Write the number down instead of guessing.

**3. Take the leave before you need it.**

Leave taken while you are still coping works. Leave taken after does not.

**4. Change one thing that repeats every week.**

The Monday meeting, the late calls, the report nobody reads.

Rest is not the same as removing the cause. You can come back from two weeks away and be tired again by Thursday.

*What have you stopped doing in the last six months without deciding to?*
