🔍 Some work needs to be right, and some only needs to be finished. Many people give the same care to both, and then have no time left for the work that is read closely.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Ask who reads this before you start.**

One named person, a committee, or nobody at all.

**2. Ask what happens if it is wrong.**

A short correction in the next email, or a customer who leaves you.

**3. Give the low risk work a time limit.**

Give it forty minutes, then send it, even when you can still see improvements.

**4. Spend the time you saved on the work that is read.**

The document your director reads deserves the third draft and the extra hour.

Not everything you write is judged closely. Deciding which pieces are judged is most of the skill.

*Which piece of work are you still improving that nobody will read closely?*
