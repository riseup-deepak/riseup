🌡 You are left off a meeting you used to attend, and your manager replies more slowly than before. Nothing is said about it, and you tell yourself you are imagining it.

*So do the opposite.*

**1. Write down what actually changed and when.**

Facts only. Dates, meetings, who replied to you and who did not.

**2. Wait two weeks and read the list again.**

One quiet week is normal. Six quiet weeks is information you can use.

**3. Ask a plain question.**

"I noticed I am not in the planning meeting now. Has something changed?"

**4. Ask before you decide what it means.**

Reorganisations, budget cuts and somebody else's bad quarter all produce the same signs.

People rarely tell you that your position has weakened. They change what they invite you to, and wait for you to work it out.

*What have you noticed at work recently and told yourself was nothing?*
